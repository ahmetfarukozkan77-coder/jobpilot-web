Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Applies to the JobPilot Chrome extension and this website.

Summary

JobPilot keeps your profile, resumes, saved answers and application history in your account on our backend (Supabase), so you can manage them on this website and use them in the extension. AI features run on our backend, which sends the data each feature needs to Anthropic's Claude models. JobPilot never submits an application for you, and it reads page content only on the tab where you use it.

Every table is protected by row-level security: a signed-in user can read and change only their own rows. The extension also keeps a copy of your profile, resumes and history in Chrome's local storage so it works quickly and offline.

What is sent to the AI model

When you use a feature, the extension sends page data to our backend, which builds the request on the server and sends it to Anthropic:

  • Reading a job posting: the visible text of that page.
  • ATS score, tailoring, cover letter: the job description and your resume text.
  • Filling a form: a summary of the form on that page — field labels, options, what is already entered (password and card fields are masked) — plus your profile, resume text and matching saved answers, which the backend reads from your account.
  • A change you type in the chat panel: your message and the form summary.

See Anthropic's privacy policy for how API data is handled. Nothing is sent to the AI model in the background: only when you start a step in the extension.

Other services

  • Supabase hosts the database, file storage, sign-in and backend functions.
  • Stripe processes payments; you pay on Stripe's own pages. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Google provides sign-in. We receive your email address and an account ID, nothing else.
  • GitHub Pages serves this website.

We do not sell data, show ads, or use tracking cookies. This website stores only your sign-in session in your browser.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete your profile, resumes, saved answers and applications on this website at any time; export your applications as CSV or JSON.
  • Turn fill statistics on or off in Settings.
  • Clear the extension's local copy by removing the extension or clearing its storage in Chrome.
  • To delete your whole account, contact the developer through the project page. Stripe billing data is managed in the billing portal (Credits → Manage billing).

Security

  • All traffic uses HTTPS; backend requests require your signed-in session.
  • AI prompts are built on the server; the extension cannot send its own instructions to the model.
  • Credit balances and usage records are written only by the backend.

Changes

We update this page when the product changes how it handles data. The date above shows the latest revision.
